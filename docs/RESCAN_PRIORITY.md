## After rescanning

1. Save the new master in `JPEG Files/` under the same filename (check no second copy with that name exists in another folder).
2. `node regen-image.js goetzmannNNNN` then `node upload-tiles.js goetzmannNNNN`, and commit the new thumbnail.
3. If the record has OCR word boxes (`scripts/ocr_boxes/`), re-run OCR for it and rebuild `scripts/corpus.db`, or its search highlights will be misplaced.
4. Re-read the record against the new scan: several descriptions note text that could not be read at the old resolution.
