# Parked significance

Interpretive content cut from labels under the facts-first rule in
[DESCRIPTION_STYLE_GUIDE.md](DESCRIPTION_STYLE_GUIDE.md). **Not discarded** — held here, keyed
by record id, for the consistency pass once the curatorial stance is set.

Two kinds of entry:

- **Cut as interpretation** — factually fine, but it argues why the object matters. Removed by
  the rule, recoverable verbatim.
- **Cut pending verification** — a load-bearing claim (named figure, specific historical
  context, which government or entity) that the verification discipline says must be confirmed
  by web search before it can be printed. **Not yet verified. Do not restore without checking.**

## goetzmann0386 — Imperial Russian 4% State Loan, 1902

✅ **Verified 2026-08-27 and restored to the label.** The loan was issued under the Ukase of
March 1, 1902 in compensation for losses from the Boxer Rebellion — Russia's share of the Boxer
Indemnity, 28.7% of 450,000,000 Haikwan taels. Sources: Spink lot CSS36000003 (described as
"1902 4% Russian State Loan (Boxer Indemnity)"), numistoria.com listing for the same issue.

⚠️ **Figure discrepancy, unresolved.** Both sources give the loan's capital as **181,959,000
rubles**; the catalogue description says **181,950,000**. I kept the catalogue's figure rather
than overwrite it from a dealer listing. Worth checking against the image.

*Cut as interpretation:*

> The multilingual denominations reveal how heavily Tsarist finance leaned on foreign capital
> markets before 1914.
